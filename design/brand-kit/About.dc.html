Our story

We build the whole yard, ourselves.

Cypress Hills started in 1994 with landscape construction and grew into a full design-build company. Today our crews handle pools, armour stone, outdoor living, lighting, putting greens and golf course shaping.

We keep the work in-house. The people who design your yard talk to the people who build it every day, and one project lead stays with you from the first site walk to the final walkthrough.
